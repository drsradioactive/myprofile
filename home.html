My Story

Built it, then modernized it

I lead cloud-native and distributed data platforms at Fidelity Investments, supporting operations across $4.5 trillion in assets, 65,000 funds, and 24,000 clients — with a rare edge: I've also modernized the mainframe systems these platforms replaced, so I understand both what we're building and what we're moving away from.

I lead a global team of 40+ engineers and senior managers, architecting on AKS and Azure, building distributed data platforms and APIs that serve high-volume, regulated environments, and owning that architecture end-to-end — from strategy through production support.

A few things I'm proud of:

  • Rebuilt and scaled API infrastructure on AKS serving 300M+ daily requests and 160,000 self-service users, with meaningfully better fault tolerance.
  • Architected distributed data and analytics reporting platforms, file transfer, and audit pipelines, handling 15,000+ files and requests daily across internal and external partners.
  • Built an end-to-end data masking pipeline across platforms, protecting sensitive and client PII in every lower environment.
  • Used ML to detect anomalies and pipeline failures before they became incidents — cutting incident rates ~15% and powering fraud alerting.
  • Modernized legacy mainframe systems (5,000+ SAS programs, 16,000 jobs) into cloud pipelines, saving $1M+ annually with zero disruption to regulated operations.

Beyond delivery, I've supported internal and external data audits, automated low-risk data changes for business teams, and trained 40+ engineers on Azure and distributed systems — because modernization only sticks if the team and the controls around it modernize together.
